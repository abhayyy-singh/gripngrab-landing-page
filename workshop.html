BACK LEVER WORKSHOP

"Back Lever — One Movement That Changes Everything"

About Workshop

The Back Lever is one of the most powerful and effective foundational movements in calisthenics — and one of the best places to begin your journey. It’s a single movement that solves multiple problems at the same time. If you have Tight Traps, try the Back Lever and feel the difference. This movement opens them up and restores ease. Shoulder pain or limited mobility? The Back Lever is completely safe when learned progressively, and it builds remarkably strong, stable, and mobile shoulders. Because it requires full-body tension and precise body-line control, the Back Lever naturally strengthens your core, supports the lower back, improves scapular mechanics, and enhances overall movement quality. Even your grip strength improves dramatically as you practice it. In this workshop, you’ll learn a progressive system — from regressions to advanced holds — teaching you how to build strength safely, fix imbalances, and develop true body control. And if you’re a fitness professional, you’ll also learn exactly how to teach this skill to your clients: what cues to give, what mistakes to avoid, how to choose the right progression, and how to identify mobility restrictions. One movement. Multiple solutions. A complete transformation in how you move and feel — starting from day one.
