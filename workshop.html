CALISTHENICS / BACK LEVER WORKSHOP

"Back Lever — One Movement That Changes Everything"

About Workshop

In this workshop, we’ll break down Calisthenics in the easiest way How to start calisthenics,what movements really matter, what people usually ignore, and how you can make steady progress. We’ll also focus on mastering one main skill: the Back Lever. It’s not just a cool move; it helps you build strong grip, a strong core, and a spine that can move better and stay healthy. This one skill becomes a base that makes your whole calisthenics journey safer and smoother. We’ll also talk about how to plan your training and answer all your personal questions, so you leave with full clarity and confidence.
